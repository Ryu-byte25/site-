QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Who is SiteVoxa for?

SiteVoxa is designed for construction companies and project teams that want a clearer way to coordinate work, information and costs.

Can we see it before subscribing?

Yes. Request a guided demo now. A self-guided sandbox workspace is also being prepared.

How does pricing work?

Plans are priced per company workspace. Annual billing includes approximately two months free compared with monthly billing.

Can we get a custom plan?

Yes. Enterprise arrangements are available for larger deployments, tailored onboarding and future integration requirements.

How are payments handled?

Checkout will be processed through a secure payment provider, while subscription verification and activation will happen on the SiteVoxa backend.

Does SiteVoxa replace our accounting system?

SiteVoxa helps teams track project budgets and operational activity. It should not be treated as a replacement for dedicated accounting software.
