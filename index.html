QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Who is SiteVoxa for?

SiteVoxa is designed for construction companies and project teams that want a clearer way to coordinate work, information and costs.

Can I see how it fits our workflow?

Yes. Request a demo and describe your team and the projects you manage. We can walk through the parts most relevant to you.

Is pricing available?

We’re speaking with early teams to understand their needs. Share your company details in the demo request and we can discuss the right arrangement.

Does SiteVoxa replace our accounting system?

SiteVoxa helps teams track project budgets and activity. It should not be treated as a replacement for dedicated accounting software.
