# Enugu Life MVP
Mobile-first Nigerian life simulator prototype. Start at UNN Nsukka with ₦4,500 and build a life through work, school, business and eventually property.

Current prototype includes persistent local saves, cash, health/hunger/energy/reputation, basic actions, travel to Enugu and a life log. Planned: careers, businesses, housing, vehicles, relationships, random events, education progression, achievements and backend accounts.

Open `enugu-life/index.html` to play.
